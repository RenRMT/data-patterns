Data smells

A data smell is the counterpart of a code smell: a surface-level sign that something may be wrong with your data.

Data smells do not immediately break a database, pipeline or spreadsheet, but they indicate an increased risk of tech debt or silent failures in the future. Fixing an issue at data entry is usually less costly than fixing it in storage, which is less costly than fixing in in a report. The later you fix it, the higher the cost and the number of people who need to know about the fix.

While a code smell points at a design problem, a data smell often points at an organisational one. If department names are entered as free text it means that nobody is responsible for the department list. In those cases the right approach is to start a conversation about data ownership, not writing a script.
